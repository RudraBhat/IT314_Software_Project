# Code of conduct

ResQ is built to help people in an emergency. We want working on it to be welcoming, respectful and safe for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or role in the project.

This code applies to everyone who takes part: maintainers, contributors, reviewers and anyone opening an issue or commenting. It covers the repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and commit messages), project chat and meetings, and any time you represent the project elsewhere.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- **Be constructive in review.** Explain the problem and suggest a way forward. Review the code, not the author.
- **Be inclusive.** Use welcoming language. Help newcomers find their way, and remember that not everyone writes in their first language.
- **Take responsibility.** Admit mistakes, fix them and learn from them, and accept feedback in the same spirit.
- **Respect privacy.** Never share real reporters' details, locations, emergency reports, account data or secrets in an issue, pull request, log, screenshot or test fixture. Use clearly labelled sample data instead.
- **Keep it about the work.** Stay on topic and keep public channels useful for everyone.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation or stalking, in public or in private messages.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, ability, appearance or experience.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Threats of violence, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission ("doxxing").
- Deliberately misusing the platform, such as false emergency reports, or sabotaging others' work.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, or continuing behaviour after being asked to stop.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, tell a maintainer privately:
- Contact the repository owner or a code owner listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS) through a private channel.
- If a maintainer is involved, contact a different one.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers review every report promptly and fairly, and may ask for more information. Depending on how serious and repeated the behaviour is, they may:

1. **Correct:** a private message explaining what was wrong, with a request to change it.
2. **Warn:** a formal warning, with consequences for further incidents.
3. **Suspend:** a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Ban:** permanent removal from the project's spaces.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. They follow it themselves too.
